About us

Queer Science is a community for LGBTQIA+ students at the Faculty of Science at the University of Copenhagen.

Our goal is to create a welcoming space where queer students can meet, support each other, share experiences, and take part in social and academic events.

Everyone who wants to help build an inclusive and respectful study environment is welcome to join our community.

What we do

  • Organize social events
  • Create community across study programmes
  • Share information and opportunities
  • Support LGBTQIA+ students at SCIENCE
